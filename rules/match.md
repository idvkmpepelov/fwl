# Match

- Two periods of 3:00. Start on knees, then the opening position is par terre.
- The lower average-ability wrestler starts both periods on top. No points for that start.
- If the match is tied, overtime restarts neutral so nobody keeps that advantage. The page uses 60 seconds. Overtime length was never fixed in the chat.
- Standing passivity: 30 seconds without a score and the more passive wrestler goes to the bottom. Par terre: 40 seconds without a score and both stand up. A score or a position change resets the timer.
- Win by pin, or on points after regulation. A 10-point lead is technical superiority: accept the win or continue.
- A pin is three uninterrupted seconds of shoulder contact inside the remaining time. A bridge or a failed second resets the count.
- Between regulation and overtime there is no recovery. Halftime recovery is `(10 + 0.3 * END) / M` fatigue points. A low-intensity ride returns 10% of that recovery per 10 seconds.
